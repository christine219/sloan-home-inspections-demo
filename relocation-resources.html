Relocation Questions, Answered

How do I find the municipality that serves my address?

Start with the parcel map and county planning or GIS department. Ask whether the property is inside municipal limits and which office handles permits, zoning, and local services. The city name used for mail may differ from the government jurisdiction.

Who provides utilities at my new home?

Confirm each service separately using the exact address. Ask the seller or property manager for current provider names, then confirm with the providers. A nearby home can have different arrangements; municipal water and sewer billing may also differ from the county provider.

Where do I find school attendance information?

Use the district’s address lookup, then confirm enrollment and assignment directly with the district. Do not assume the nearest school serves the home.

Does a flood map replace an inspection or insurance review?

These resources serve different purposes. Maps help you research flood information; your home inspection evaluates accessible systems and components. Discuss coverage and property-specific flood questions with your insurer and local floodplain office.

What should I check if the home has a private well or septic system?

Confirm the system type, available records, maintenance history, and any transaction requirements. Ask our team about well water testing and septic inspection coordination before closing.

Can Sloan help me find the right starting point?

Yes. Our team can help you navigate this guide and explain inspection options for your property. Utility providers, schools, and local agencies confirm their own service areas, applications, and requirements.
