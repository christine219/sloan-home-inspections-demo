Purchasing a home with
a government-backed loan?

Do you know which additional services your lender needs? Start with your property type and ask for the lender's written checklist.

Standard Homes

  • Well Water Testing: Test the water quality of the home’s independent water supply system. Confirm the required laboratory panel and timing with your lender.
  • Septic Inspection: Ask whether the property's septic system needs an inspection or health authority documentation. We coordinate services through Coastal Septic.

Manufactured Homes

  • HUD / Manufactured Home Foundation Certification: Ask your lender whether an engineer’s certification is required.
  • Well Water Testing: Test the water quality of the home’s independent water supply system. Confirm the testing requirements with your lender.
  • Septic Inspection: Confirm the requested scope for an on-site septic system; we coordinate with Coastal Septic.
Schedule early. Get clarity before closing. Requirements depend on the loan program, lender, property, and appraisal findings. These services are not automatically required for every government-backed loan. Unsure which inspections your lender needs? That’s where we can help. Does an appraisal replace a home inspection?

No—they answer different questions.

Appraisal: What is the home worth? The appraiser estimates the property’s value for the lender. With government-backed financing, the appraisal may also identify conditions that need to be addressed for the loan.

Home inspection: What condition is the home in? Your inspector takes a closer look at accessible systems and components—such as the roof, plumbing, electrical, heating, and cooling—to document visible concerns and maintenance needs.

An appraisal can help your lender evaluate the loan. A home inspection helps you understand the home you’re buying. An appraisal does not replace a home inspection, and a home inspection does not determine property value or guarantee loan approval.
